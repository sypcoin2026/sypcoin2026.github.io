H28 · 6 NOVEMBER 2026 · MATHEMATICAL MODEL VERIFIED

Two independent integer algorithms checked 4,599,000 emission heights with zero mathematical mismatches. Base and FRM budgets are exhausted at height 4,599,000.

Scope: The model's first FRM payment is 642 nanoSYPC at height 1,051,201. This does not validate native Scala block admission, autonomous monetary contracts or post-emission blocks.

Public evidence pending.

What each label actually means.

Overall development status remains red regardless of individual passed tests.

The design or component exists in part but is not sufficiently validated.

UNDER TEST

Some tests may succeed while mandatory coverage or integration is still missing.

LAB VERIFIED

The described local case passed. This is neither a production launch nor an external security audit.
