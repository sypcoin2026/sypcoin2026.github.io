MONETARY POLICY

Issuance by rules.
No hidden allocation.

The experimental genesis includes initial allocations to solidarity, development and mining reward funds alongside PoW issuance. A blanket zero-premine claim would therefore be misleading. No personal founder reserve is planned; the final monetary distribution and consensus rules remain unratified.

PrinciplesControlsStudiesEvidence

MODEL

Goals for fair initial distribution.

The policy is a design commitment pending final specification and consensus validation.

EXPERIMENTAL GENESIS

Declared initial funds

The laboratory model includes initial allocations to solidarity, development and mining incentives. These are distinct from mined issuance and are not final mainnet allocations.

NO PERSONAL FOUNDER RESERVE

Separate fund purposes

No personal founder allocation is planned. Solidarity and development funds have distinct purposes, while custody and spending controls remain unimplemented.

VERIFIABILITY

Independent supply verification

A node must be able to reconstruct authorized issuance from validated chain history, not from claims by a project operator.

CONSENSUS VALIDATION

How issuance must be checked.

Emission-related components should be tested with valid, invalid and adversarial inputs.

GENESIS

Reproducible initial state

The definitive genesis must expose the initial distribution and reject hidden or unrecognized outputs.

BLOCK REWARDS

Exact issuance rules

Each reward is bounded by block height, monetary parameters and valid contract conditions.

UTXO

Issuance state tracking

Transitions must remain correct during synchronization, conflicting transactions and possible reorganizations.

NOT FINAL

Parameters under evaluation.

Exploratory economic models do not constitute activated consensus changes.

EMISSION HORIZON

Long-term schedule

The experimental schedule targets approximately 35 years, 240 seconds per block and contributions to the mining reward fund beginning in year eight. These values remain subject to validation and final ratification.

SUPPLY

Cap and curve

The final supply ceiling and emission shape will need a versioned specification, code review and boundary tests.

FRM

Mining Reward Fund

Experimental FRM models examine mining incentives over time. These are not public-network rules yet.

LAB RESULTS

What has passed in experiments.

Tests document limited components, not the complete monetary protocol.

SYP-LAB-001

Accounting and genesis

9/9 monetary accounting tests and a PASS for experimental genesis integration.

SYP-LAB-002

ErgoTree and interpreter

5/5 contract tests and 8/8 interpreter tests under laboratory conditions.

SYP-LAB-003

UTXO emission processing

5/5 transaction builder, 5/5 candidate generator and 6/6 tracking tests.

Important limitation

There is no published operational SYPcoin genesis or independent chain supply. Proposed monetary rules should not be presented as completed consensus activation.
