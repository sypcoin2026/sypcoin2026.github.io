MINING / GPU EFFICIENCY

GPU mining.
Efficiency measured, not promised.

SYPcoin targets vendor-agnostic GPU Proof-of-Work. On the new private P0 chain, an RX 7900 XTX/Linux mined H1–H3 with SOAT/Autolykos2; two nodes accepted H2/H3 and recovered H3 after restarting. Public and long-running mining, Windows and other GPU vendors remain unverified; no official miner is available.

CURRENT LAB BASIS

Autolykos2 under evaluation.

The earlier Ergo devnet GPU experiment was not native SYPcoin mining. In v41.3 the experimental Autolykos2 GPU path on RX 7900 XTX/Kubuntu produced one native SYPcoin block at height 1, accepted and persisted by two private JVM nodes (H12). There is no public miner. In v41.4-R4 a valid height-2 PoW block was accepted and persisted by two private nodes; independent GPU attribution remains unverified.

LIVE RESULTS · PRIVATE P0 CHAIN

SOAT Async V2. H2 and H3 mined.

The experimental Vulkan/Autolykos2 engine with asynchronous candidate reception mined H2 and H3 on a new private P0 chain. Two nodes verified both blocks and their UTXO state. These results are distinct from historical v41.x experiments.

H32 · INTEGRATED MINING

Bounded AMD/Linux measurement

During H2, an RX 7900 XTX averaged 183.32 MH/s over 320 seconds, with a peak sample of 188.80 MH/s. H3 measured 158.74 MH/s for only 10 seconds, not a sustained result. H32 limits.

H33 · AUDIT AND RESTART

H3 state recovered

Cold audit and two-node restart recovered H3 with the matching UTXO root from isolated copies. No new mining occurred during these checks. This is not proof of 24/7 uptime. H33 limits.

PENDING · NOT FIELD VERIFIED

Continuity and safe supervision

H4–H10 has not been run. The H3+ controller passed 19/19 offline tests, but its guardian has not been physically validated during prolonged mining. Power readings above the configured limit remain under investigation.

ROADMAP · NOT CERTIFIED HARDWARE

AMD and NVIDIA. Linux and Windows.

The consensus protocol will accept valid PoW regardless of GPU vendor. Actual miner compatibility depends on drivers, APIs, VRAM, compute capabilities and cooling: not every graphics card can be promised support.

01 · VERIFIED IN PRIVATE LAB

AMD RX 7900 XTX · Kubuntu/Linux

One native GPU-mined height-1 block accepted by two private SYPcoin nodes in v41.3. No public release or sustained benchmark.

02 · NOT YET VALIDATED

AMD RX 7900 XTX · Windows

Next: test the miner on the same AMD GPU under Windows, then study optional energy optimization. No confirmed results yet.

03 · NOT YET VALIDATED

NVIDIA RTX 3060 · Windows

After AMD/Windows, develop and validate NVIDIA mining first, then any optional tuning profiles. NVIDIA/Linux follows in later testing.

04 · FUTURE · NO HARDWARE TEST

Intel and other GPUs

Study when suitable physical cards and driver support are available. No blanket vendor or old-GPU compatibility certification is claimed.

MINING CLIENT · IN DEVELOPMENT

One separate mining engine

A single SOAT/Autolykos2-based engine is planned to receive work and submit candidates. Its Terminal PRO and graphical interfaces are not yet integrated or available for download.

SYP EcoMiner · OPTIONAL CONCEPT

External per-device optimization

A future tool to measure hashes per joule (hashes/J), power, temperatures and stability, offering conservative configurable AMD/NVIDIA profiles according to OS and permissions. Overclocking is prohibited in the project's miner development; there is no validated public download.

PRODUCT DESIGN · NO MINER AVAILABLE

One engine.
Two planned interfaces.

SYPcoin Miner is planned around a single SOAT/Autolykos2 engine with two optional interfaces. Terminal PRO would be integrated through an experimental fork of SOAT while retaining traditional console use. The graphical interface would consume the same engine data through a safe supervisor. These are not two separate miners.

Independent local Terminal PRO v0.1/v0.2 Python prototypes exist with panels and read-only monitoring, but they are not integrated into SOAT. The GUI is only a visual concept. SYPminer is the chosen name for a monitoring application derived from Terminal PRO and separate from the SOAT engine; its real telemetry integration is not yet verified and no public download exists.

No download or autonomous mining yet

Continuous SOAT integration, safe supervision, candidate refresh, autonomous issuance and ASERT remain incomplete. Real applications must show verified live metrics (MH/s where appropriate), prohibit overclocking in development and enforce thermal safety and explicit consent.

MEASUREMENT FRAMEWORK

How energy efficiency should be evaluated.

An experimental read-only AMDGPU monitoring tool passed 6/6 offline CI tests (H26). It reports sensors but has not adjusted GPU settings, mined native SYPcoin or demonstrated real energy savings. Hashrate alone is not accepted work.

Private P0 H1–H3 mined; no public miner

Height 1 was mined on AMD RX 7900 XTX/Linux and retained by two private SYPcoin nodes (H11/H12). After offline signing and height-2 PoW verification, v41.4-R4 confirmed that the valid height-2 PoW block was accepted and persisted by two private nodes. Independent GPU attribution for that historical test remains unverified. Separately, SOAT Async V2 mined private P0 H1–H3, with H3 recovered by both nodes; this is not long-running operation. No public mining software, pool, or proven Windows/NVIDIA/Intel support exists.
