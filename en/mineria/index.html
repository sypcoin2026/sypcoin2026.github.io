MINING / GPU EFFICIENCY

GPU mining.
Efficiency measured, not promised.

The project's objective is open, GPU-oriented Proof-of-Work participation. Energy efficiency, stability and block verification must be tested together. There is currently no public SYPcoin mining endpoint or official mining package.

GoalsExperimental basisMeasurementsPublic availability

DESIGN PRINCIPLES

Mining that can be measured.

Optimization cannot compromise consensus or hide the real operating cost.

GPU

Graphics hardware

Evaluate practical mining on consumer GPUs while studying risks of specialized hardware advantage.

ENERGY

Work per unit of power

Separate device readings from whole-system energy consumption and state measurement conditions.

RELIABILITY

Sustained operation

Measure accepted work, stability, thermal limits and recovery from errors over comparable test periods.

CURRENT LAB BASIS

Autolykos2 under evaluation.

Ergo 6.0.8 and compatible GPU tooling are used as an experimental foundation only.

01

Ergo devnet experiments

A controlled local setup allows mining and node behavior to be tested, but it is not a public SYPcoin chain.

02

Actual GPU block attribution

A convincing result must identify a newly produced block, independently validated by nodes, rather than relabeling a pre-existing block.

03

Future algorithm choice

Mining design and parameter choices remain subject to security, energy, fairness and implementation reviews.

MEASUREMENT FRAMEWORK

How energy efficiency should be evaluated.

A displayed hashrate alone does not prove accepted work or economical participation.

POWER

Measured consumption

State GPU-reported power, whole-machine consumption if measured, duration and the measurement method.

OUTPUT

Verifiable work

Track valid shares or newly accepted blocks, with the attribution and confirmation conditions made clear.

THERMALS

Hardware safety

Monitor temperature, hotspot, memory, fan and driver behavior. Unverified voltage or frequency tweaks are not public recommendations.
