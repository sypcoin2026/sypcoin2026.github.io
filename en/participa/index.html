HOW TO HELP

Three ways to contribute.

Current opportunities focus on public technical discussion and website quality.

DEVELOPERS

Review assumptions

Discuss monetary invariants, consensus testing, version evolution and maintainable modular architectures.

GPU MINERS

Evaluate methods

Suggest accurate approaches to measure accepted blocks, system power, thermal stability and hardware compatibility.

COMMUNITY

Improve documentation

Report broken links, explain confusing passages, review mobile usability and suggest better translations.

OPTIONAL SIGNUP

We only need your email.

No name, password, wallet or financial details. Notifications will be sent manually from the project mailbox.

This temporary system does not verify that you own the email address. Submissions reach a private mailbox, not public GitHub.

Controller: SYPcoin project management. Purpose: the requested notification; legal basis: consent. Web3Forms processes the submission and forwards it to the project's private inbox. Data is kept until the notice and at most 30 days after it, unless you withdraw earlier. Privacy information.

If the automatic service fails, choose “Send from my email instead”. Your email app opens a prepared message; you still must press “Send”. If it does not open, email sypcoin@proton.me directly. Signup is not guaranteed or confirmed until received.
