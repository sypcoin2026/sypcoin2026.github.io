8 November 2026H32

Private testnet progress: asynchronous mining and block recovery

SYPcoin (SYPC) mined blocks H2 and H3 with SOAT Async V2 on a private chain. Two nodes accepted them and verified their state; H3 was recovered after both nodes restarted. During H2, an RX 7900 XTX averaged 183.32 MH/s for 320 seconds.

Limited private demonstration, not sustained performance or a public network. H4–H10, SYPminer and physical guardian testing are not verified; no downloadable miner exists.

7 November 2026H31

First live block on the new P0 private testnet

An RX 7900 XTX running Linux mined H1 on a new SYPcoin (SYPC) private P0 chain. Two nodes accepted it and recovered the same block and state after restarting.

First block of this private instance, not a public testnet or proof of sustained mining. Later blocks and ASERT remained untested at that date.

SYPcoin Miner: two planned interfaces, one engine

The design for Terminal PRO and a graphical interface sharing one SOAT/Autolykos2 engine has been approved. Local terminal prototypes exist; integration and a working GUI remain pending.

Product design decision, not a release or proof of sustained mining. Images are fictional mockups; no public miner is available.

6 November 2026H30

Akka candidate lifecycle verified in a laboratory

The focused Scala/Akka v0.15.1 test passed 1/1: a test Ergo block was applied, stale mining work was rejected and a new candidate was checked after forced regeneration.

Local Ergo test only; not proof of continuous automatic refresh, GPU mining, P2P, autonomous issuance or a public testnet.
