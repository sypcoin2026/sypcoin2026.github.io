EVIDENCE PUBLICATION

Checks before releasing reports.

Evidence must be genuine, sufficiently contextualized and safe to publish.

IDENTITY

Version, date and success criteria

Record the precise component, measured condition, environment and expected outcome.

PRIVACY

No confidential identifiers

Remove private credentials, local paths, private IPs and personal or sensitive third-party data.

INTEGRITY

Real public files and checksums

Only publish checksums calculated from the actual released evidence, with URLs that were verified to exist.

LIMITATIONS

Explain what was not shown

Clearly distinguish local passes, public testnet evidence and third-party security assessments.

Primary evidence not yet published

Summary cards must not be mistaken for the original laboratory logs or audit reports. Links will be added only after a genuine source is available and safe for public use.
