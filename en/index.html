SYPcoin is an experimental independent cryptocurrency project. We are developing a modular protocol, GPU-oriented mining, security testing and verifiable monetary rules, using Ergo 6.0.8 as a technical foundation.

Important: pre-testnet laboratory stage

There is no operational SYPcoin public testnet, mainnet, official wallet or public mining service. Laboratory results based on Ergo devnet do not constitute a launched SYPcoin network.

DOCUMENTED PROGRESS

First native GPU-mined SYPcoin block.
Verified in a private lab, not public mining.

In v41.3 an AMD RX 7900 XTX on Kubuntu/Linux produced native height-1 SYPcoin PoW, accepted and retained by two private JVM nodes. V41.4-R2 signed a height-2 candidate offline, and R3 passed 10/10 focused tests, but block 2 has not been mined or accepted. Full consensus and public mining are still pending. AMD/Windows and NVIDIA/Windows are future targets.
