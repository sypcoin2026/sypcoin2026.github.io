8 November 2026H32

Private testnet progress: asynchronous mining and block recovery

SYPcoin (SYPC) mined blocks H2 and H3 with SOAT Async V2 on a private chain. Two nodes accepted them and verified their state; H3 was recovered after both nodes restarted. During H2, an RX 7900 XTX averaged 183.32 MH/s for 320 seconds.

Limited private demonstration, not sustained performance or a public network. H4–H10, SYPminer and physical guardian testing are not verified; no downloadable miner exists.

DOCUMENTED PROGRESS

Three private P0 blocks mined.
Verified in a laboratory, not a public network.

A new private P0 chain mined H1–H3 with AMD RX 7900 XTX/Linux and SOAT/Autolykos2. Two native nodes accepted H2/H3 and recovered H3 after a controlled restart. During H2, SOAT Async V2 recorded an average 183.32 MH/s for 320 seconds; this is not sustained operation over hours. Earlier v41.x experiments remain separate. Full consensus, public mining and Windows/NVIDIA compatibility are pending.
